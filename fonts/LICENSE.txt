Fonts used in this project: Lora and Source Sans 3.

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.
Full license text: https://openfontlicense.org

- Lora — designed by Cyreal. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Lora
- Source Sans 3 — designed by Paul D. Hunt (Adobe). https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Source+Sans+3

Files were downloaded as static WOFF2 subsets (Latin + Latin Extended, to
cover Polish diacritics) via Google Fonts and are self-hosted in this
repository under fonts/lora/ and fonts/source-sans/ — no runtime dependency
on Google Fonts or any other CDN.
