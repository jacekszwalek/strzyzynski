Nałęczów Stanisława Strzyżyńskiego

This virtual walk follows four sculptures by Stanisław Strzyżyński (1923–2015), which have shaped the landscape of Nałęczów for decades. The recordings are narrated by his son, Zbigniew Strzyżyński, who tells the story of his father and his work. You can take the whole walk from home before visiting Nałęczów, or step by step on site by scanning the QR codes by each sculpture. The route has four stops — you'll find the exact distance and estimated walking time below the map.
