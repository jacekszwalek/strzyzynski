Introduction

Before you set off

Narrated by Zbigniew Strzyżyński, the artist's son
Expand the transcript

Hello. My name is Zbigniew Strzyżyński — I am a sculptor, painter, and also an educator, a former deputy director for artistic affairs at the State Secondary School of Fine Arts in Nałęczów.

I am also president of the Sztuka i Dom Foundation, established to commemorate the creative legacy of my father, the sculptor Stanisław Strzyżyński — a man of great merit to Nałęczów, to the Lublin region, and to Poland as a whole. Today I will tell you about him, and about several of his works found in Nałęczów.

    The artist

    Stanisław Strzyżyński (1923–2015)

    Youth and education

    Stanisław Strzyżyński was born on 3 April 1923 in Kaliska (Greater Poland Voivodeship). He studied at the Sculpture Department of the State Higher School of Fine Arts in Poznań, in the studio of Prof. Bazyli Wojtowicz. He graduated from the Academy of Fine Arts in Warsaw in 1955, in the studio of Prof. Marian Wnuk.

    Nałęczów

    He moved to Nałęczów in 1953. For thirty years, from 1953 to 1983, he worked as a sculpture teacher at the State Secondary School of Fine Arts in Nałęczów. He belonged to the Union of Polish Visual Artists from 1951, and in 1957–1974 he co-founded the Nałęczów Group. Together with the painter Jarosław Olejnicki, chairman of the Society of Friends of Nałęczów (Towarzystwo Przyjaciół Nałęczowa), he organised national plein-air sculpture workshops: "Park Sculpture" in 1975 and "Figures of Nałęczów" in 1979.

    Body of work

    The artist's body of work includes twenty monuments, a dozen or so outdoor sculptures, around a hundred portrait sculptures, several dozen medals, as well as sacred sculptures and works on martyrological themes.

    In Nałęczów he created, among others, the Bolesław Prus Bench, a monument dedicated to the soldiers of the Home Army (Armia Krajowa) who fell fighting the NKVD, the Maximilian Kolbe monument, figures of Our Lady of the Mystical Rose, the Merciful Christ and the Christ of Sorrows, as well as the outdoor sculptures "Family Tree", "The Maiden with a Fish" and "Children's Dance".

    Beyond Nałęczów

    Beyond Nałęczów, Strzyżyński created, among other works, the twenty-three-metre Monument to the Heroic Airmen of the Dęblin Eaglets' School in Dęblin (1966), a six-metre figure of Christ the Saviour in Warsaw, as well as monuments in Rąblów, Kock, Zbędowice and Puławy, and benches dedicated to Maria Cunitz in Świdnica, Józef Ignacy Kraszewski in Romanów, and Stefan Żeromski in Siedlce.

    Awards and honours

    In 1967, together with Juliusz Kłeczek, he won first prize in the national competition for the Monument to Struggle and Martyrdom at Majdanek. He was decorated with, among others, the Officer's Cross and the Knight's Cross of the Order of Polonia Restituta, as well as the Gold Cross of Merit.

    He died on 16 March 2015 in Nałęczów.

    In June 2026, an alley in the Spa Park in Nałęczów was named after him.

    Foundation

    The initiative "Cyfrowy spacer śladami artysty rzeźbiarza Stanisława Strzyżyńskiego" ("A digital walk following the sculptor Stanisław Strzyżyński") is carried out as part of the "Fundusz Inicjatyw MRW – Działam, bo lubię" project, funded by the Fundusz Inicjatyw Międzynarodowego Roku Wolontariatu. The Regional Operators of the project are: Bieszczadzki Uniwersytet Ludowy, Stowarzyszenie Sursum Corda, and Fundacja Bieszczadzka.
