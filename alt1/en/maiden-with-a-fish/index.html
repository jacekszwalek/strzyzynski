The Maiden with a Fish at Ewelina Café

Year 1979
Material artificial marble
Location Fountain in front of the "Pod Matką Boską" villa, home to the Ewelina café
Author Stanisław Strzyżyński

The Maiden with a Fish, placed in the fountain in the garden of the well-known and much-loved Ewelina café, was created in 1979. It was commissioned by the café's owner at the time, Ewelina Bartosiewicz. My father had already made designs for the Maiden with a Fish back in the 1950s. Photographs of the small plaster designs of the sculpture have also survived.

In the garden you can also see many other sculptures, both outdoor pieces and, inside, portrait busts. Among others, he sculpted the owner, Ewelina Bartosiewicz. There is also a head of Władysław Tatarkiewicz, whom he sculpted from life, it was from this very head that he later made the larger-than-life bust now in the Palm House in the Spa Park.

If you walk a little further along Lipowa Street, you'll see Bolesław Prus Lane on the right. That is where Stanisław Strzyżyński's house and studio are located.

If you would like to stop by the studio and see the sculptures up close, you're welcome to get in touch by phone and arrange a visit. See you at Stanisław Strzyżyński's studio.

Foundation

The initiative "Cyfrowy spacer śladami artysty rzeźbiarza Stanisława Strzyżyńskiego" ("A digital walk following the sculptor Stanisław Strzyżyński") is carried out as part of the "Fundusz Inicjatyw MRW – Działam, bo lubię" project, funded by the Fundusz Inicjatyw Międzynarodowego Roku Wolontariatu. The Regional Operators of the project are: Bieszczadzki Uniwersytet Ludowy, Stowarzyszenie Sursum Corda, and Fundacja Bieszczadzka.

Funded by the National Freedom Institute – Centre for Civil Society Development, as part of the International Year of Volunteers Initiatives Fund.
